Zach's Crazy Meeting Rules to Live By

Crazy meeting rules I follow because I actually want people to get work done:

01

No agenda? I'm assuming we don't need the meeting.

02

A status update is not a meeting.

03

25 minutes is usually enough for a 30-minute meeting.

04

If two people need to solve something, 10 people don't need to be invited to watch/listen.

05

"Optional" should actually mean optional.

06

If you need me to make a decision, tell me that before the meeting.

07

If you need me to be prepared to discuss a specific technical capability, tell me that before the meeting.

08

I'm not scheduling a meeting to discuss the meeting we just had.

09

If we leave without an owner and a next step, we mostly just talked.

10

Recurring meetings have to keep earning their spot on the calendar.

11

Sometimes the most productive thing I can do is decline.
